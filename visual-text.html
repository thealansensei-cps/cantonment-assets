Read the Heading First

Always read the title, main heading and any subheadings before the questions. This tells you the topic and purpose.

🔍

Locate Key Details

For factual questions (dates, prices, venues, names), scan the text carefully. Always go back to check — don't rely on memory.

❓

Understand the Question

Read the question fully before looking at options. "Purpose" questions ask why — think about what the ad is trying to achieve.

🚫

Eliminate Wrong Answers

Cross out clearly wrong options first. When two seem similar, find the one that is exactly supported by the text — not just close.

✅

True / Not True Questions

Check every option against the text. Watch for options that are almost right but have one wrong detail — these are traps!

🖼

Purpose of Pictures

Think about how images attract attention or support the main message. Their purpose usually connects to the ad's overall goal.
