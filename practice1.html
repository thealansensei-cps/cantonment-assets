Section E: Synthesis & Transformation
Rewrite using the key word given (4 × 1 mark)
Q29
Many tourists visit Hawaii. They can see beautiful beaches there.
→ Rewrite as one sentence using: where
✅ Model Answer Many tourists visit Hawaii, where they can see beautiful beaches.
📌 Grammar Rule – Relative Clause with "where"
Use where to join two sentences when the second sentence describes a place. The relative clause introduced by "where" must come directly after the place noun (Hawaii).
Q30
If you do not buy a ticket, you will not be able to enter the theme park.
→ Rewrite as one sentence using: unless
✅ Model Answer You will not be able to enter the theme park unless you buy a ticket.
📌 Grammar Rule – "unless" = "if… not"
Unless means "except if" — it replaces if … not. Drop the word "not" from the original condition when using unless.
Q31
The television is new. The oven is new too.
→ Rewrite as one sentence using: Both
✅ Model Answer Both the television and the oven are new.
📌 Grammar Rule – "Both … and …"
Both … and … links two subjects of equal importance. The verb must be plural (are, were, have) because both subjects are included.
Q32
They said, "We are going for our swimming lesson this afternoon."
→ Rewrite as reported speech using: They said that
✅ Model Answer They said that they were going for their swimming lesson that afternoon.
📌 Grammar Rule – Reported Speech Backshift
We → they  |  are → were  |  our → their  |  this afternoon → that afternoon
When changing to reported speech: pronouns shift (we/our → they/their), present tense shifts to past (are → were), and time expressions change (this → that).
