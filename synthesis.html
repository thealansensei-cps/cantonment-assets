Text 1 — Blog Post
Many students in Singapore spend too much time on screens. Research shows that children aged 7 to 12 spend an average of four hours a day on devices. Experts say this can affect sleep, eyesight and concentration. Parents are encouraged to set screen time limits to protect their children's health.
Text 2 — Forum Reply
